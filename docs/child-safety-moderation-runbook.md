# Child-safety and UGC moderation runbook

**Child Safety Point of Contact:** Masterminds Support Team — mindm1517@gmail.com

This runbook is for authorised Master Minds moderators. It is an operational checklist; the publisher must ensure the mailbox is monitored and reviewers are assigned before release.

## Intake

- Review reports in the authorised moderation portal. The in-app report control is the preferred route because it includes the report ID and content reference.
- For reports received by email, record the account username, report type, description, date received and reporter contact in the moderation queue. Do not ask the reporter to send suspected illegal images or videos.
- Treat child-safety concerns as priority reports. Keep access to the report and any retained evidence limited to authorised reviewers.

## Initial response

1. Confirm that the report points to a real account or content item.
2. If the report indicates an immediate risk, restrict access to the relevant content or account while a qualified reviewer assesses it.
3. Do not download, duplicate, forward, or attach suspected child sexual abuse material. Do not ask a child to send additional images or details.
4. Preserve only the account identifiers, report metadata, relevant timestamps and other information needed for a lawful investigation or required report. Use approved secure storage and access controls.
5. Record every action through the audited moderation workflow, including the reviewer, action, reason and time.

## Child exploitation material or grooming concerns

- Remove or disable access to prohibited material as soon as the report is substantiated or actual knowledge is obtained.
- Restrict accounts involved in suspected grooming, exploitation, sextortion, trafficking or other conduct that endangers a child.
- Escalate to the designated Child Safety Point of Contact immediately.
- Report to the appropriate competent authority in the relevant jurisdiction where required by applicable law. Confirm the current local reporting obligation with qualified counsel or the relevant authority; do not assume one country's reporting process applies everywhere.
- Cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities. Do not disclose reporter or child information publicly.

## Other UGC reports

- Review harassment, bullying, threats, spam, privacy violations and inappropriate content.
- Remove content or apply proportionate account restrictions when a violation is confirmed.
- A user may block another account from supported social and communication surfaces. Verify that the block takes effect for messages, friend requests, follows, multiplayer invitations and Study Mode calls.
- Do not dismiss a report solely because the reported user is a friend, school member or staff member.

## Account restrictions and appeals

- Use the audited moderation actions for content removal, suspension or bans.
- Add a concise reason. Do not place passwords, verification codes or unnecessary personal details in the note.
- Review an appeal through an authorised reviewer who was not the original decision-maker where practical.
- Do not restore access until the safety concern has been reviewed and the account status has been intentionally changed.

## Release readiness checklist

- The support mailbox is monitored and has a named on-call reviewer.
- Moderators can access the report queue and the report context needed to review each supported content type.
- Moderators know how to remove content, suspend or ban accounts, and record actions.
- The Child Safety Standards page is public and linked from the app and privacy/terms pages.
- The publisher has confirmed the applicable local child-safety reporting route and any required legal escalation process.
- Play Console declarations about target age, UGC, privacy, data safety and child safety match the app's actual behaviour.
